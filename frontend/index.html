🎾 Cherry's Hobbies

Cherry is an energetic Golden Retriever who loves to play and explore! Here are her favorite activities:

🎾

Playing Fetch

Absolutely loves chasing tennis balls! Can play for hours without getting tired.

Favorite!
🏃

Running

Enjoys morning runs in the park. Gets super excited when she sees the leash!

💦

Swimming

True to her breed, Cherry loves water! Splashing in puddles or swimming in ponds.

Favorite!
🦴

Chewing Toys

Has a collection of chew toys. The squeaky ones are her absolute favorite!

🐕

Playing with Other Dogs

Very social and friendly. Loves making new friends at the dog park.

🛋️

Cuddling

After playtime, loves to snuggle on the couch with family members.

🚗

Car Rides

Gets excited for car trips! Loves sticking her head out the window.

🌳

Exploring Nature

Enjoys sniffing around gardens and exploring new trails on walks.

🎯

Tug of War

Loves playing tug with rope toys. Never gives up easily!

⚠️ Activity Note

Due to Cherry's PSS condition, we keep activities moderate and ensure she doesn't overexert. Short play sessions with rest breaks work best!
